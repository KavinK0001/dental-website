Treatments

Four core specialties, each handled with detailed diagnosis and a painless-first approach.

Root Canal Treatment

For infected or badly decayed teeth, root canal treatment removes the damaged pulp, cleans the canal, and seals the tooth — saving it rather than extracting it. Handled with careful pain management throughout.

Jaw Pain Treatment

Treatment for jaw stiffness, clicking, or TMJ-related pain, including wisdom-tooth related discomfort — often resolved in a single, efficient, low-pain visit.

Tooth Fixation

Repair for chipped, cracked, loose, or injured teeth — including urgent same-day care for sudden dental injuries.

Cosmetic Dental Procedures

Whitening, shaping, and alignment corrections for a more confident smile, planned around each patient's specific teeth.
